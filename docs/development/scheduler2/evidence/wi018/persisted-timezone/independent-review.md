Staging is input-proportional outside mutations. Hot validation restores only the fixed89-byte header and addresses at most16 pages of at most230 encoded bytes per install/cleanup batch. Semantic fingerprint and definition identity are distinct; positive monotone generations prevent identical replacements from reusing page owners. Replacement retains exactly the installed old prefix under a FIFO descriptor. Every addressed cleanup page is validated before any deletion; new owners cannot overtake unfinished older owners. UTC has zero pages and remains a distinct explicit constructor; empty custom bytes are not silently interpreted as UTC.
