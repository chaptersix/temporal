## Contract and dependencies

Public configured capacity, including disabled MaxBufferSize, remains supported. Two coordinator quanta alone are insufficient.

Depends on accepted CHASM/WI-001 guarantees and M2 contracts, plus current WI-015 implemented public execution/capacity/timestamp checkpoints. **Does not depend on WI-015 acceptance**, avoiding a cycle.
