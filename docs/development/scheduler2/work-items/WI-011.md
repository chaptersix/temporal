   task, keyed result and lifecycle transition. Map them to independently specified
   transitions and named internal stutters. Identify state projections, immutable
   inputs, task/recovery obligations and fairness assumptions. Model-to-Go mapping
   tables must distinguish checked correspondence from manual review.
2. Resolve the local atomic update contract from reference/source evidence.
   ConfigureGeneration and decision.Update in separate transactions are not an
   atomic UpdateSchedule. Specify a coherent local transaction boundary for source,
   policy, pause and budget replacement, preserving queued identity/policy,
   admission-time payload/carryover, and already frozen start/effect requests.
   Implement missing behavior only after the contract is recorded.
3. Exercise composed update cases: paused/resumed and exhausted/replaced budget,
   early completion and failure carryover, queued policy, bound effects, concrete
   vacancy leases, jittered automatic pending supersession and historical backfill
   rebase. Reject publication at update and handoff boundaries; check the complete
   cursor/lease/task/request projection after reload. Retain saved deterministic
   traces and independent expected observations; a seed alone is insufficient.
4. Reconcile C1–C11, S1–S8, L1–L3 and SP obligations against actual evidence.
   Explicitly classify workflow-ID reuse, external dedup horizons and
   continue-as-new/reset chain identity assumptions. Public authentication/API
   execution belongs to M3, but an undefined local chain contract cannot be hidden
   behind that boundary.
5. Map F1–F8 historical regressions and implemented events to current tests and
   generated/replayed coverage. Distinguish short UTC generated calendar cases
   from pinned DST examples and M3 full boundary-biased spec-processing checks.
6. Audit P3.3 instrumentation: preparation/deserialization, modified nodes,
   serialized bytes and persistence reads. Add missing required measurements or
   identify precisely why a measurement belongs at a later integration boundary.
   Poisoned DATA proves avoided decoding, not avoided database reads. Large-scale
   load and growth comparisons remain M4.
